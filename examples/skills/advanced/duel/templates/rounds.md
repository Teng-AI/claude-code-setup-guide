# Round instructions

Read `packet.md` (and `evidence.md` if present) first, then the files your round names. Write your answer as markdown. Stay under the word limit.

## Every round: how to change your mind

- **Concede only to an argument or a piece of evidence, never to the other analyst's confidence or tone.** When you keep a position the other attacked, say in one line why their argument doesn't move you.
- **Conceding just to reach agreement is a failure.** A clear record of one real disagreement is worth more than a proposal both of you half believe.
- **When you change a position, log it** in a "Position changes" table: what changed, from, to, and the exact argument or evidence that caused it.

## round0 (blind, about 1,200 words)

You haven't seen the other analyst's work. Write your own proposal with these sections, in this order:

1. **Recommendation in two lines.**
2. **Assumptions:** a table with id, assumption, fact or guess, source or reasoning, and what changes if it's wrong.
3. **Options considered:** one row per option on the menu and any you add, with a verdict (adopt, adopt in part, defer, reject) and a one-line reason.
4. **The proposal:** answer every question the packet lists, as a table where it fits.
5. **Measurement:** what to measure, and the result that would change the plan.
6. **Risks:** the three that worry you most.
7. **What would change my mind.**
8. **Open questions:** evidence you'd want and don't have.

## swap (about 1,200 words; this is the only exchange)

Read the other analyst's round0 (named in your prompt). Write:

1. **Where we agree** (a short list).
2. **Where we disagree:** a table with the point, their position, yours, and your argument.
3. **What their proposal gets wrong or leaves out** that matters most.
4. **Position changes** (the table above, or "none").
5. **Checkable facts in dispute:** facts that a source could settle (a price, a rule, a number), each with the source that would settle it. Don't argue them; list them.
6. **Your revised proposal:** section 4 from round0, updated.

## final (about 500 words)

Read `owner.md` (the owner's earlier decisions, shown only now so they couldn't anchor you) and `facts-checked.md` if present. For each point where your revised proposal differs from an owner decision or a checked fact: **hold or defer**, and why, in one line each. A preference stated without evidence is not a reason to defer.
